About Bestcare

Leading provider of drainage and exhauster services in Nairobi, Kenya

Bestcare Exhauster Services specialises in exhauster services, septic tank pumping, drain unblocking, hydro jetting, CCTV drain surveys, biodigester construction and maintenance, grease trap cleaning, and general drainage maintenance — all at affordable prices.

N

NEMA Certified Fleet

Modern fleet of vacuum exhauster trucks handled by skilled professionals. All trucks are vetted and certified by NEMA, so every job complies with national environmental regulations. Waste always disposed at licensed treatment facilities.

S

Safe & Compliant

Every job includes sealed transport, GPS-tracked vehicles, and — for commercial clients — a disposal manifest recording volume extracted, disposal site licence, vehicle registration, date, and operator, suitable for NEMA, county health, and insurance records.

50+

Towns Covered

Deployed fleet on the ground across Kenya's major urban and peri-urban centres, with guaranteed response times — not just listed locations.
