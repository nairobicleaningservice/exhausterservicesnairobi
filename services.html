Key services offered by Bestcare

Professional exhauster providers do far more than simply empty tanks. Understanding these services helps property owners choose the right type and frequency of maintenance.

1

Septic Tank Emptying and Pumping

Core service. Vacuum tanker connects hose to septic tank access point and suctions out all accumulated sludge, scum, and liquid waste. Tanks serviced range from 500L household to 20,000L+ commercial. Frequency: 1-3 years homes.

2

High-Pressure Hydro Jetting

After emptying, high-pressure water jetting cleans inside walls and connected drain pipes. Removes biofilm, grease, hardened solids. Clears blocked drainage lines restoring flow.

3

Septic System Inspection

Checks inlet/outlet baffles, tank walls and lid, drain field or soak pit, structural damage or groundwater contamination. Written inspection report.

4

Bio-Treatment and Odour Control

Biological enzyme treatments or bacterial activators introduce helpful microorganisms that speed breakdown of organic waste, lengthen interval between exhausting sessions, reduce odours.

5

Grease Trap Cleaning

Restaurants, hotels, schools, food-handling facilities use grease traps to keep fats, oils, grease out of main drainage. Jet-vac extraction, thorough cleaning, service certificate.

6

Drain Unblocking and Root Removal

Blocked drains common in every property. Mechanical augers, flexible drain rods, high-pressure jetting to break grease, hair, soap, foreign objects, invading tree roots.

7

Soak Pit Rehabilitation

Soak pits clog over time due to excess solids, root intrusion, soil saturation. Rehabilitation involves emptying, inspection, excavation and rebuilding to restore absorption capacity.

8

CCTV Drain Surveys

CCTV camera inspections identify blockages, leaks, root intrusion, structural damage without excavation. Accurate, non-invasive, cost-effective.

9

Drainage and Sewer Repair

Long-lasting solutions for blocked, damaged, or leaking sewer lines, using advanced techniques such as CCTV inspection and trenchless repair.

10

Biodigester Construction, Maintenance, and Repair

Eco-friendly alternative, constructs efficient biodigesters for homes, businesses, institutions. Biodigesters decompose waste naturally and reduce need for frequent exhausting.

11

Plumbing Services

Reliable plumbing for residential, commercial, industrial: leak repairs, pipe installations, drain unblocking, toilet and sink repairs, water tank installations, emergency plumbing.

"Many property owners only think about their septic systems when something goes wrong. Proactive maintenance through regular exhausting and inspection is far less expensive than emergency call-outs and repairs." — Bestcare

What affects your exhauster price?

  • Tank volume: main cost driver — more litres mean larger truck or more trips
  • Waste type: liquid effluent costs less than thick sludge, grease, industrial waste
  • Hose distance: access points far from road need extended hoses; keep truck within 30m
  • Disposal distance: distance to NEMA-licensed facility affects transport cost
  • Emergency vs scheduled: after-hours same-day carry 20–30% surcharge
  • Contract discount: scheduled contracts 10–15% off ad-hoc rates
