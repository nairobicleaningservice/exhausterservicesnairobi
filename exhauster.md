## What Are Exhauster Services?

Exhauster services are the professional emptying, pumping, cleaning, and maintenance of septic tanks, soak pits, biodigesters, pit latrines, and other wastewater containment systems. Using specialised vacuum tanker trucks, trained technicians pump out accumulated sludge and effluent, then transport the waste in sealed tanks to approved disposal or treatment facilities.

In Nairobi, the high density of estates, commercial buildings, and institutions that depend on on-site sanitation means exhauster services are needed on a regular basis. Without timely emptying, septic systems overflow, causing contamination, foul smells, health risks, and costly damage to property.

> "A well-maintained septic system is the foundation of a healthy and hygienic property. Regular exhausting and cleaning should not be treated as a luxury but as an essential part of property management." — Bestcare Exhauster Services

In Kenya, exhauster services are required wherever a property is not connected to a public sewer — which covers most homes, businesses, and industries outside Nairobi's CBD and major town centres. Even sewer-connected properties often keep on-site waste systems that need periodic servicing.

## Key Services Offered by Bestcare Exhauster Services

Professional exhauster providers do far more than simply empty tanks. Understanding these services helps property owners choose the right type and frequency of maintenance.

### 1. Septic Tank Emptying and Pumping

This is the core service. A vacuum tanker truck connects its hose to the septic tank access point and suctions out all accumulated sludge, scum, and liquid waste. Tanks serviced range from small household units of 500 litres to large commercial installations above 20,000 litres. How often a tank needs emptying depends on its size, the number of users, and how quickly waste builds up — usually every one to three years for homes.

### 2. High-Pressure Hydro Jetting

After emptying, technicians often use high-pressure water jetting to clean the inside walls of the tank and connected drain pipes. This removes stubborn biofilm, grease, and hardened solids that ordinary pumping cannot dislodge. Jetting also clears blocked drainage lines across a property, restoring flow in partially or fully obstructed pipes.

### 3. Septic System Inspection

A thorough inspection checks the inlet and outlet baffles, the condition of the tank walls and lid, the state of the drain field or soak pit, and any signs of structural damage or groundwater contamination. Bestcare Exhauster Services issues a written inspection report — valuable documentation for property owners, buyers, and health authorities.

### 4. Bio-Treatment and Odour Control

After exhausting, technicians may apply biological enzyme treatments or bacterial activators to the freshly emptied tank. These products introduce helpful microorganisms that speed up the breakdown of organic waste, lengthen the interval between exhausting sessions, and reduce odours.

### 5. Grease Trap Cleaning

Restaurants, hotels, schools, and other food-handling facilities use grease traps to keep fats, oils, and grease out of the main drainage system. Bestcare Exhauster Services provides jet-vac grease trap extraction, thorough cleaning, and a service certificate for health inspection compliance.

### 6. Drain Unblocking and Root Removal

Blocked drains are a common problem in every kind of property. Technicians use mechanical augers, flexible drain rods, and high-pressure jetting to break up and remove blockages caused by grease, hair, soap, foreign objects, or invading tree roots. Prompt unblocking prevents sewage backup and damage to pipework.

### 7. Soak Pit Rehabilitation

Soak pits clog over time due to excess solids, root intrusion, or soil saturation. Rehabilitation involves emptying, inspection, and sometimes excavation and rebuilding of the pit or its perforated lining to restore its absorption capacity.

### 8. CCTV Drain Surveys

CCTV camera inspections identify blockages, leaks, root intrusion, and structural damage without excavation. This accurate, non-invasive method supports cost-effective repairs and is available from Bestcare Exhauster Services at affordable rates.

### 9. Drainage and Sewer Repair

Bestcare Exhauster Services delivers long-lasting solutions for blocked, damaged, or leaking sewer lines, using advanced techniques such as CCTV inspection and trenchless repair to restore drainage systems.

### 10. Biodigester Construction, Maintenance, and Repair

Biodigesters decompose waste naturally and reduce the need for frequent exhausting. Maintenance and repair services keep these systems efficient, odour-free, and long-lasting.

## How Often Should You Use Exhauster Services?

The right frequency depends on the tank size, the number of users, whether a garbage disposal unit is connected, and the age of the system. Recommended schedules by property type are:


| Property Type | Recommended Schedule | Notes                                                                                                              |
| ------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Residential   | Every 1–3 years      | A family of four in a correctly sized tank should pump every 2–3 years; larger families should target 12–18 months |
| Commercial    | Every 6–12 months    | Offices, guesthouses, SMEs; high-occupancy offices may need quarterly service                                      |
| Industrial    | Monthly              | Factories, processing plants, and large institutions on volume contracts                                           |
| Hospitality   | Monthly              | Monthly grease trap cleaning; hotel septic tanks quarterly or biannually depending on occupancy                    |


Warning signs that a tank needs immediate attention include slow-draining sinks and toilets, gurgling sounds in the pipes, foul odours indoors or around the drain field, and visible sewage surfacing in the yard.

### What Affects Your Exhauster Price?

- **Tank volume:** The main cost driver — more litres mean a larger truck or more trips.
- **Waste type:** Liquid effluent costs less to handle than thick sludge, grease, or industrial waste.
- **Hose distance:** Access points far from the road need extended hoses and longer set-up; keep truck access within 30 metres of the tank where possible.
- **Disposal distance:** The distance to the NEMA-licensed disposal facility affects transport cost.
- **Emergency versus scheduled:** After-hours and same-day call-outs carry a 20–30% priority surcharge.
- **Contract discount:** Scheduled maintenance contracts attract 10–15% off ad-hoc rates.
